Report engine: spending (or income) by Category for any period, with drill-down into children and
shares that add up to exactly 100%. Only income and expense Lines count. Transfers and balance
adjustments are left out, and a Transfer's fee counts as spending. Foreign Lines convert at their
own frozen rate, so a past period never changes. Debt principal will be left out once Debts exist.
