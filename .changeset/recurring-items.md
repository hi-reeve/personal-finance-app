Recurring items: templates for bills, income and standing transfers (every N days, weeks, months
or years, with an end date or count, month-end clamping and a last-day option). Occurrences are
computed; only paid or skipped ones are stored under a deterministic id. Mark paid, link an
existing Transaction or Transfer, skip, reopen, and skip all older, with overdue occurrences kept
until acted on and expected-versus-paid shown.
